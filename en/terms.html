Booking terms and policies

General terms for booking, payment, changes and cancellation of our day tours. The specific conditions of each tour are on its own page.

How to book

To book, we need the following information for each passenger:

  • Full name
  • RUT or passport number
  • Date of birth
  • Nationality
  • Address
  • Contact phone number
  • Dietary restrictions (if applicable)

Payment:

  • Marble Chapel & Cathedrals and Queulat National Park: 50% of the tour total must be paid to confirm the booking. The remaining 50% should ideally be paid the day before, or directly to the tour guide.
  • San Rafael Lagoon: To confirm the booking, the full price of the tour must be paid in cash, by bank transfer, or by debit or credit card.
  • Ensenada Pérez Hot Springs: To confirm the booking, full payment is required, by electronic transfer, bank deposit or cash.

Road tours

Marble Chapel & Cathedrals and Queulat National Park.

The tour date can be changed by notifying us up to 3 days before the agreed date. The new date will depend on the agency's availability.

Cancellations must be notified 7 days before the tour, in which case 100% of the amount paid will be refunded. If the cancellation is notified less than 7 days in advance, the deposit is non-refundable.

If a passenger does not show up or cannot be reached on the day of the tour, the deposit will not be refunded.

If the agency cancels the tour because of adverse weather, it can be rescheduled or 100% of the amount paid refunded.

Marble Chapel & Cathedrals only: If the port is closed, a percentage of the total price will be refunded, corresponding to the cost of the boat.

Please note that all tours are subject to weather conditions. The weather in the Aysén Region is unpredictable and changes greatly from one place to another.

Catamaran tours

San Rafael Lagoon and Ensenada Pérez Hot Springs.

  • Departure and arrival times may vary depending on weather conditions.
  • Not recommended for people with recent injuries or for women more than 7 months pregnant.
  • The excursion is operated by the agency Loberías del Sur, owner of the catamarans.
  • There are 3 rates for this excursion: C1, C2 and C3. Rate C1 applies to low season, C2 to mid season and C3 to high season. The rate may also vary depending on seat availability on the catamaran at the time of booking.
  • Passengers who are vegetarian, vegan, celiac or allergic to any product must tell us when booking.

Cancellations must be made in writing, and the passenger accepts the penalty set out below.

Mid-high season: if the cancellation is notified 45 days before the start of the excursion, the total amount paid will be refunded, minus 10% for administrative costs. If it is notified between 45 and 20 days before the start, 50% of the total amount paid will be refunded. With less than 20 days notice, there is no refund.

If a passenger wishes to cancel a booking in order to make a new one taking advantage of a promotion published after the original booking, no refund of any kind applies to the deposit paid. Promotions are valid from the date they are published and are never retroactive.

If the passenger(s), because of rain or other natural conditions that do not prevent one or more of the contracted activities from taking place, do not wish to take the excursion, no refund of any kind applies.

If the passenger does not show up or cannot be reached on the day and at the time the excursion starts, non-participation is assumed and no part of the service price is refunded.

If the sea excursion is suspended because of weather conditions, it can be rescheduled to a new date subject to availability.

If departure is suspended because of technical failures, the excursion can be rescheduled to another day or the money refunded: if payment was made by transfer or cash, the full amount is refunded; if it was made by credit or debit card, the amount paid is refunded minus a 5% Redcompra commission.

Only if the company is responsible for the excursion not taking place will the passenger be refunded the full price of the land or sea excursion that was not carried out. The refund will be made within 10 business days. Because of the high demand for this excursion, boarding tickets are sent by email within 4 business days after full payment.

By paying for or making a deposit on the excursion, the passenger is understood to have read and accepted these policies and conditions.

San Rafael Lagoon has additional clauses, which are on the tour page.

All tours are subject to weather conditions.
