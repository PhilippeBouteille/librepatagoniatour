Day tour

Water Route

Half day · 5 hrs

The Water Route of Puerto Aysén is a scenic drive that showcases the region's abundance of water, through rivers, waterfalls, cascades and natural viewpoints.

Details

This tour explores several points of interest near the city where water is the main character: waterfalls, rapids, deep pools and green landscapes that reflect the essence of Patagonia.

It is an accessible and rewarding experience, ideal for discovering the natural identity of Puerto Aysén and enjoying the strength and beauty of water in its purest state.

Price

Tour price: $45.000 CLP per person.

Stops along the route

  • Marchant Viewpoint.
  • Velo de la Novia Waterfall.
  • La Virgen Waterfall.
  • Presidente Ibáñez Bridge.
  • Salto El León.
  • Puerto Aysén Plaza.

What's included
  • Transfer from Coyhaique to Puerto Aysén, departing between 07:00 and 07:30.
  • Guide throughout the tour.
  • Photo stops at the main attractions along the Carretera Austral.
  • Visit to the Velo de la Novia, La Virgen and Salto El León waterfalls.
  • Return to your home between 15:00 and 15:30.

Things to know
  • Lunch is not included: there is a stop at the Copec service station for the restroom and to buy food.
