Day tour

Marble Chapel & Cathedrals

Full day · 12 hrs

The Marble Chapels nature sanctuary is a national monument of Chile. It sits on the western shore of General Carrera Lake in the Aysén Region, near Puerto Río Tranquilo, 215 kilometers from the regional capital, Coyhaique. It is one of the most visited attractions among travelers who come to our region.

Details

On the way from Coyhaique to Puerto Río Tranquilo we pass several natural and tourist attractions, such as Cerro Castillo National Park, which protects a variety of wildlife including the huemul, puma, guanaco, Patagonian hog-nosed skunk and culpeo fox, among others.

The tour includes several photo stops until we reach General Carrera Lake, where, during the boat trip on the lake, you will see the Cathedral, the Cavern and the Marble Chapel.

The tour starts and ends in the city of Coyhaique. Departure is between 05:00 and 05:30, with arrival back at approximately 20:30.

Price

Tour price: $85.000 CLP per person.

What's included

  • Pick-up from your home or accommodation in Coyhaique and transfer to the Marble Chapels.
  • Boat trip through the marble chapels, caverns and caves.
  • Regional driver-guide.
  • Photo stops at viewpoints and points of interest.
  • Lunch at local restaurants, with products from their own production.

How to book

To book, we need the following information for each passenger:

  • Full name
  • RUT or passport number
  • Address of each passenger (home or accommodation)
  • Dietary restrictions (if applicable)

50% of the tour total must be paid to confirm the booking. The remaining 50% should ideally be paid the day before, or directly to the tour guide.

Things to know
  • There is a stop at a rustic café, typical of our Carretera Austral, where passengers can buy breakfast. It is important to use the restroom here, as there are no more facilities until Puerto Río Tranquilo.
  • The lunch menu is fixed (home-style cooking). It can only be changed for passengers with dietary restrictions: vegetarian, vegan or celiac.
  • Drinks, breakfast and snacks are not included.
  • Of the 215 km to Puerto Río Tranquilo, 108 km are paved road and 107 km are gravel.
  • Departure and arrival times may vary depending on weather conditions.

Terms and policies

The tour date can be changed by notifying us up to 3 days before the agreed date. The new date will depend on the agency's availability.

Cancellations must be notified 7 days before the tour, in which case 100% of the amount paid will be refunded. If the cancellation is notified less than 7 days in advance, the deposit is non-refundable.

If a passenger does not show up or cannot be reached on the day of the tour, the deposit will not be refunded.

If the agency cancels the tour because of adverse weather, it can be rescheduled or 100% of the amount paid refunded.

If the port is closed, a percentage of the total price will be refunded, corresponding to the cost of the boat.

Please note that all tours are subject to weather conditions. The weather in the Aysén Region is unpredictable and changes greatly from one place to another.
