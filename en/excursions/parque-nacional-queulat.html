Day tour

Queulat National Park

Full day · 12 hrs

Queulat National Park is part of the enveloping green Patagonian forest, which we proudly recognize as an emblem of our beloved Patagonia. Inside the park we find native forests, waterfalls, fjords, rivers and lagoons, with the millennial Ventisquero Colgante (Hanging Glacier) as its main highlight.

Details

The route starts in the regional capital, Coyhaique (Aysén Region), and heads north along the Carretera Austral until, 200 km later, we reach Queulat National Park. Along the way you will see highlights such as Villa Mañihuales, Las Torres Lake, Mirador del Valle, the Cisnes River, the Piedra del Gato Viaduct, Salto El Cóndor, Queulat Fjord and the Puyuhuapi Channel.

Once at the destination, we take a low-intensity trek of about 25 minutes that crosses a suspension bridge over the Ventisquero River and ends at Laguna Témpano, where you can see the Hanging Glacier and its waterfall. An optional boat trip can also be added, not included in the tour price ($15.000 per person).

The tour starts and ends in the city of Coyhaique, with pick-up at your home or accommodation from 06:00 and arrival back at approximately 20:30.

Price

Tour price: $85.000 CLP per person.

What's included

  • Pick-up from your home or accommodation in Coyhaique.
  • Regional driver-guide.
  • Photo stops at viewpoints and points of interest.
  • Lunch at local restaurants, with products from their own production.

How to book

To book, we need the following information for each passenger:

  • Full name
  • RUT or passport number
  • Address of each passenger (home or accommodation)
  • Dietary restrictions (if applicable)

50% of the tour total must be paid to confirm the booking. The remaining 50% should ideally be paid the day before, or directly to the tour guide.

Things to know

  • The lunch menu is fixed (home-style cooking). It can only be changed for passengers with dietary restrictions: vegetarian, vegan or celiac.
  • Drinks, breakfast and snacks are not included.
  • The optional boat trip is not included in the tour price.
  • Departure and arrival times may vary depending on weather conditions.

Terms and policies

The tour date can be changed by notifying us up to 3 days before the agreed date. The new date will depend on the agency's availability.

Cancellations must be notified 7 days before the tour, in which case 100% of the amount paid will be refunded. If the cancellation is notified less than 7 days in advance, the deposit is non-refundable.

If a passenger does not show up or cannot be reached on the day of the tour, the deposit will not be refunded.

If the agency cancels the tour because of adverse weather, it can be rescheduled or 100% of the amount paid refunded.

Please note that all tours are subject to weather conditions. The weather in the Aysén Region is unpredictable and changes greatly from one place to another.
