Day tour

San Rafael Lagoon

Full day · 15 hrs
Home/Day Tours/San Rafael Lagoon

This tour is a unique catamaran sailing experience to San Rafael Lagoon, located inside San Rafael Lagoon National Park, in the Aysén Region.

During the trip, passengers sail through channels and Patagonian landscapes until they reach the imposing San Rafael Glacier, where one of the most memorable moments of the tour takes place: tasting whisky with ancient ice taken directly from the glacier.

The experience lets you see up close the eternal ice, the glacier's calving and an almost untouched natural setting.

Day itinerary

  • Departure days: Monday, Friday and Saturday.
  • Departure time: from 05:30 AM, from your accommodation within the urban area of Coyhaique.
  • Arrival back in Coyhaique: from 10:00 PM.
  • Check-in time: 07:00 AM at Puerto Chacabuco. Arrival back at Puerto Chacabuco: 9:00 PM.

The rate may vary depending on catamaran availability on the day of booking.

Rates

  • Adults: $354.000
  • Seniors over 60: $323.000
  • Children 4 to 11: $255.000

Additional transfer Coyhaique–Puerto Chacabuco–Coyhaique: $45.000 per person.

What's included
  • Catamaran boarding ticket.
  • Zodiac excursions (weather permitting).
  • Tour guide.
  • A toast with whisky and millennial ice, bar service (on the return, visit to the national park).
  • Tea service, hot snacks and sweets during the return sail.
  • Breakfast, lunch and dinner.

To book, we need the following information for each passenger:

  • Address

To confirm the booking, the full price of the tour must be paid in cash, by bank transfer, or by debit or credit card.

Things to know
  • Departure and arrival times may vary depending on weather conditions.
  • Not recommended for people with recent injuries or for women more than 7 months pregnant.
  • The excursion is operated by the agency Loberías del Sur, owner of the catamarans.
  • There are 3 rates for this excursion: C1, C2 and C3. Rate C1 applies to low season, C2 to mid season and C3 to high season. The rate may also vary depending on seat availability on the catamaran at the time of booking.
  • Passengers who are vegetarian, vegan, celiac or allergic to any product must tell us when booking.
