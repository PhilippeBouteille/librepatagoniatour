Day tour

Queulat National Park + Ventisquero Hot Springs

Full day · 12 hrs

This full-day tour combines nature, hiking and relaxation in one of the most emblematic areas of the Aysén Region.

Details

The experience begins with a visit to Queulat National Park, where trails run through evergreen forest, along rivers and to natural viewpoints, with the impressive Ventisquero Colgante (Hanging Glacier) as the highlight.

The day continues at the El Ventisquero hot springs, whose natural thermal waters let you relax and recharge in a unique Patagonian setting, closing the day with well-being and rest.

The tour starts and ends in the city of Coyhaique, with departure between 07:00 and 07:30 towards Queulat Park, in Puerto Puyuhuapi, and return to your home between 20:00 and 20:30.

Price

Tour price: $135.000 CLP per person.

What's included
  • Transfer from Coyhaique.
  • Guide throughout the tour.
  • Photo stops at the main attractions along the Carretera Austral.
  • Entrance ticket to Queulat National Park and 3 hours at the Ventisquero hot springs.
  • Lunch at a restaurant (drinks not included).

How to book

To book, we need the following information for each passenger:

  • Full name
  • RUT or passport number
  • Address of each passenger (home or accommodation)
  • Dietary restrictions (if applicable)

50% of the tour total must be paid to confirm the booking. The remaining 50% should ideally be paid the day before, or directly to the tour guide.

Things to know
  • Drinks are not included.
  • Departure and arrival times may vary depending on weather conditions.

Terms and policies

The tour date can be changed by notifying us up to 3 days before the agreed date. The new date will depend on the agency's availability.

Cancellations must be notified 7 days before the tour, in which case 100% of the amount paid will be refunded. If the cancellation is notified less than 7 days in advance, the deposit is non-refundable.

If a passenger does not show up or cannot be reached on the day of the tour, the deposit will not be refunded.

If the agency cancels the tour because of adverse weather, it can be rescheduled or 100% of the amount paid refunded.

Please note that all tours are subject to weather conditions. The weather in the Aysén Region is unpredictable and changes greatly from one place to another.
