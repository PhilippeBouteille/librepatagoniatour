Day tour

Horseback Ride + Country Tea

Half day · 5 hrs

This tour offers an adventure and nature experience in a privileged setting near Puerto Aysén.

Details

The activity begins with a guided horseback ride along natural trails, through native forests and Patagonian landscapes, letting you explore the area in a calm, traditional way, with views of the rivers, mountains and valleys typical of the region.

After the ride, the experience continues with a country-style tea (once campestre), where all participants share and enjoy a moment of rest in a natural setting, closing the day in a warm and welcoming way.

Price

Tour price: $65.000 CLP per person.

What's included
  • Transfer from Coyhaique to Puerto Aysén (variable schedule).
  • Guide throughout the tour.
  • Guided horseback ride.
  • Photo stop with the horses in the countryside.
  • Country-style tea.
