Day tour

Horseback Ride + Country Tea

Half day · 5 hrs

We travel toward Puerto Aysén along Route 240. This guided circuit follows the banks of the Simpson River through wooded trails, taking in the abundant and extensive flora and fauna of the area, with stops to enjoy the scenery. Before starting, there is an instructional talk and the necessary equipment is provided.
