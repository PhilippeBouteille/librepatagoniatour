Chapter II · Coyhaique — Caleta Tortel

Southern Carretera Austral and the City of the Césares

6 days / 5 nights · Coyhaique to Caleta Tortel

Season opens October 2026. Only 2 departures per month, limited spots.

This 6-day expedition follows the southern Carretera Austral between Balmaceda Airport and Coyhaique. It begins by crossing Cerro Castillo National Park with a stop at the Río Ibáñez Waterfall, then sails Lago General Carrera from Puerto Ibáñez to Chile Chico, with its waterfront and the Cerro de Las Banderas. We continue along the Paso Las Llaves Scenic Route, through mountains and steppe, to Puerto Guadal and Cochrane. From there we reach Caleta Tortel, with its cypress boardwalks, and sail to the historic Isla de los Muertos. The route continues through Parque Patagonia and the confluence of the Baker and Neff rivers, then sails from Puerto Río Tranquilo to the Capillas de Mármol Nature Sanctuary, closing with the final transfer to Balmaceda Airport.

See full itinerary

Day 1

Balmaceda · Cerro Castillo · Puerto Ibáñez · Chile Chico

Reception at Balmaceda Airport, heading south through Cerro Castillo National Park with a stop at the Río Ibáñez Waterfall. Sailing across Lago General Carrera to Chile Chico. Overnight in Chile Chico.

Day 2

Chile Chico · Paso Las Llaves · Puerto Guadal · Cochrane

We tour the Chile Chico waterfront and the Cerro de Las Banderas. The Paso Las Llaves Scenic Route to Puerto Guadal and Cochrane. Overnight in Cochrane.

Day 3

Cochrane · Caleta Tortel · Isla de los Muertos

A guided walk along the cypress boardwalks of Caleta Tortel and a sailing to the historic Isla de los Muertos. Overnight in Caleta Tortel.

Day 4

Parque Patagonia · Baker–Neff Confluence · Lago Negro

Trails, viewpoints and conservation projects in Parque Patagonia, and the confluence of the Baker and Neff rivers. Overnight in Lago Negro.

Day 5

Puerto Río Tranquilo · Marble Chapels · Coyhaique

Sailing on Lago General Carrera to the Marble Chapels Sanctuary: the Chapel, the Cathedral, the Caves and the Caverns. Return to Coyhaique. Overnight in Coyhaique.

Day 6

Coyhaique · Balmaceda Airport · Santiago

Transfer from the hotel to Balmaceda Airport, concluding the journey along the southern Carretera Austral.

Highlights

Cerro Castillo and Patagonia Parks and the Marble Chapels Sanctuary; sailing on Lago General Carrera and to Isla de los Muertos; the Paso Las Llaves Scenic Route; Caleta Tortel and Chile Chico; wildlife including pumas, condors, huemul deer, foxes and eagles.
