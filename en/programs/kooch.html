Chapter III · Chile — Argentina

Kooch, The Creator of Patagonia

13 days / 12 nights · Chile and Argentina

There is a place in the world where ancient glaciers meet turquoise rivers, where native forest embraces remote fjords, and the Andes unfold in their wildest form: Patagonia. This exclusive 13-day, 12-night program invites you to travel the entire Carretera Austral, sail through austral channels and fjords, and cross into Argentine Patagonia to admire the legendary Perito Moreno Glacier and the Torres del Paine up close. An expedition designed for travelers seeking nature, adventure and local culture in their purest form, with the constant support of a professional Libre Patagonia Tour team.

See full itinerary

Day 1

Santiago · Balmaceda · Cerro Galera · Coyhaique

Landing at Balmaceda Airport. A visit to Fundo Don Gerardo and Monumento Patagonia, and a Patagonian Gaucho Experience with spit-roast asado. Overnight in Coyhaique.

Day 2

Cerro Castillo · Marble Chapels · Lago Negro

Cerro Castillo National Park with stops at the Portezuelo Ibáñez viewpoint, the Río Murta Valley and El Cofre. Sailing on Lago General Carrera to the Marble Chapels. Overnight at Lago Negro (Puerto Guadal).

Day 3

Puerto Bertrand · Parque Patagonia · Cochrane

Puerto Bertrand, birthplace of the Baker River, and its confluence with the Neff River. Crossing Parque Nacional Patagonia (Tompkins Conservation). Overnight in Cochrane.

Day 4

Caleta Tortel · Crux Australis Sailing

Caleta Tortel and its cypress boardwalks, sailing to Isla de los Muertos. At dusk we board the Crux Australis vessel bound for Puerto Natales. Overnight on board.

Day 5

Crux Australis Sailing

A full day sailing past Puerto Edén, austral fjords and channels, the Falsas Torres rock formations, and marine wildlife colonies. Overnight on board.

Day 6

Puerto Natales

Disembarkation and free time in Puerto Natales, by the Última Esperanza Sound. Overnight in Puerto Natales.

Day 7

Puerto Natales · El Calafate (Argentina)

Border crossing into Argentine Patagonia. Arrival in El Calafate, by Lago Argentino. Overnight in El Calafate.

Day 8

Perito Moreno Glacier (Argentina)

Sailing through Los Glaciares National Park admiring the Upsala and Spegazzini Glaciers, followed by the Perito Moreno Glacier boardwalks. Overnight in El Calafate.

Day 9

Torres del Paine National Park

Laguna Azul with views of the Torres del Paine, a chance to spot pumas on the way to Portería Amarga, the Lago Pehoé viewpoint, Salto Grande and Lago Nordenskjöld. Overnight in the Lago Grey area.

Day 10

Grey Glacier · Milodón Cave · Puerto Natales

Sailing aboard the Grey III catamaran among icebergs, and a visit to the Milodón Cave National Monument. Overnight in Puerto Natales.

Day 11

Balmaceda and Serrano Glaciers · Punta Arenas

Sailing the Última Esperanza Fjord past the Serrano Glacier, with a thousand-year-old-ice whisky and a Patagonian lamb asado at Estancia Perales. Overnight in Punta Arenas.

Day 12

Fuerte Bulnes · San Isidro Lighthouse · Punta Arenas

A walk through Magellanic forest to the San Isidro Lighthouse, facing the Strait of Magellan, and a visit to Fuerte Bulnes. Overnight in Punta Arenas.

Day 13

Punta Arenas City Tour · Santiago

Plaza Muñoz Gamero, the Cerro de la Cruz viewpoint, Sara Braun Cemetery and the Nao Victoria Museum. Transfer to the airport and flight to Santiago.

Highlights

Cerro Castillo, Patagonia, Los Glaciares (Argentina) and Torres del Paine Parks; Upsala, Spegazzini, Perito Moreno, Grey, Balmaceda and Serrano glaciers; the Crux Australis ferry through channels and fjords; Milodón Cave, Fuerte Bulnes and San Isidro Lighthouse; a single Chile–Argentina border crossing on day 7.
