Chapter IV · Coyhaique — Cochrane — Caleta Tortel

Marble Chapels + Caleta Tortel + Patagonia National Park

3 days / 2 nights · Coyhaique, Cochrane and Caleta Tortel

This program offers a complete journey through Southern Patagonia, combining nature, culture and iconic landscapes of the Aysén Region. It includes the boat trip to the Marble Chapels Nature Sanctuary, a walk along the boardwalks of Caleta Tortel and a visit to Patagonia National Park, departing from and returning to Coyhaique.

See full itinerary

Day 1

Coyhaique · Puerto Río Tranquilo · Marble Chapels · Cochrane

Departure from Coyhaique between 7:00 and 7:30 AM toward Puerto Río Tranquilo, where we sail to the Marble Chapels Nature Sanctuary, one of Chile's most emblematic natural wonders. After the boat trip, lunch at a restaurant and onward to the town of Cochrane, where we spend the night.

Day 2

Cochrane · Caleta Tortel · Isla de los Muertos

Departure from Cochrane between 7:00 and 7:30 AM bound for Caleta Tortel. On arrival we walk its wooden boardwalks, typical of this timber-based (not fishing) settlement and unique in Patagonia. During our stay there is the option of visiting Isla de los Muertos, subject to schedule availability. Lunch at a restaurant, then back to Cochrane for lodging and rest.

Day 3

Patagonia National Park · Baker–Neff Confluence · Coyhaique

Visit to Patagonia National Park (formerly Douglas Tompkins National Park), including its museum on the history and conservation of the area. A short trek takes us to the confluence of the Baker and Neff rivers, a spot of great natural beauty. We then drive to Puerto Río Tranquilo for lunch and end the day in Coyhaique, arriving between 8:00 and 8:30 PM.

Highlights

Marble Chapels Sanctuary, the wooden boardwalks of Caleta Tortel, Isla de los Muertos (optional), Patagonia National Park and the confluence of the Baker and Neff rivers.
