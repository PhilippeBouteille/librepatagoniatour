Chapter I · Carretera Austral Norte

Explorers and Settlers

7 days / 6 nights · Coyhaique to Chaitén

Season opens October 2026. Only 2 departures per month, limited spots.

This 7-day expedition explores Northern Patagonia from a base in Coyhaique. It begins at Balmaceda International Airport and Fundo Don Gerardo, where we learn about the Monumento Patagonia conservation project and experience a Patagonian Gaucho afternoon with traditional spit-roast asado. From Puerto Chacabuco we sail to Laguna San Rafael National Park and its glacier, among fjords and mountains, with a toast of thousand-year-old glacier ice. We continue along the Carretera Austral Norte to Queulat National Park and the Hanging Glacier, followed by a hot-spring experience before reaching La Junta. The route continues to the Yelcho Glacier viewpoint and Chaitén, with a stop at the Airplane Monument, before returning via El Amarillo and its hot springs to La Junta. We close by passing through Puerto Aysén and the Presidente Ibáñez Bridge, back to Coyhaique, where the expedition ends.

See full itinerary

Day 1

Balmaceda Airport · Gaucho Experience · Coyhaique

Reception at Balmaceda International Airport. We visit Fundo Don Gerardo and the Monumento Patagonia project, and enjoy a Patagonian Gaucho Experience with spit-roast asado before transferring to Coyhaique. Overnight in Coyhaique.

Day 2

Sailing to San Rafael Lagoon and Glacier

Departure for Puerto Chacabuco, where the sailing to Laguna San Rafael National Park begins. We sail up to the San Rafael Glacier, with meals on board and a toast with thousand-year-old ice, among the fjords and mountains of Aysén. Overnight in Coyhaique.

Day 3

Queulat · Hanging Glacier · Hot Springs · La Junta

We head to Queulat National Park, crossing temperate rainforest and visiting the Hanging Glacier, followed by a hot-spring experience on the way to La Junta. Overnight in La Junta.

Day 4

Yelcho Glacier · Carretera Austral · Chaitén

A hike to the Yelcho Glacier viewpoint, then on to Chaitén with a stop at the Airplane Monument. Overnight in Chaitén.

Day 5

Chaitén · El Amarillo · Hot Springs · La Junta

Heading back north, visiting El Amarillo and its trails, with the option of enjoying the El Amarillo hot springs. Overnight in La Junta.

Day 6

La Junta · Puerto Aysén · Coyhaique

A stop in Puerto Aysén to see the Presidente Ibáñez Bridge and various viewpoints. Arrival in Coyhaique. Overnight in Coyhaique.

Day 7

Coyhaique · Balmaceda Airport · Santiago

Transfer from the hotel to Balmaceda International Airport, closing an expedition through glaciers, fjords, forests, national parks and hot springs.

Highlights

Laguna San Rafael and Queulat National Parks, Monumento Patagonia; Puerto Chacabuco–San Rafael sailing; San Rafael, Hanging and Yelcho glaciers; Airplane Monument and Presidente Ibáñez Bridge; Gaucho Experience with spit-roast asado; Ventisquero and El Amarillo hot springs.
